# Team Ideation

## Brainstormed Ideas
1. **AI Code Review & Refactoring Assistant** – A lightweight web tool that analyzes pasted code snippets for bugs, security issues, and readability improvements.
2. **AI Meeting Action-Item Extractor** – Converts messy meeting transcripts into structured task lists with assigned owners and deadlines.
3. **AI Study & Flashcard Generator ✅ (Chosen)** – Transforms lecture notes, articles, or PDF text into active-recall flashcards and short practice quizzes.

## Problem Statement
Students and self-learners spend hours passively rereading dense notes instead of actively testing their knowledge. Manually creating quality flashcards and practice questions is time-consuming, leading to inefficient study sessions and poor retention. An AI-powered tool can instantly convert raw study material into structured active-recall exercises.

## MVP Features
- **Notes Input:** Users can paste raw text or upload plain-text study notes.
- **Automated Flashcard Generation:** AI extracts key concepts and generates question-and-answer flashcard pairs.
- **Interactive Quiz Mode:** Users can flip through flashcards and take a quick 5-question multiple-choice quiz based on the material.
- **Export Option:** Download generated flashcards in CSV or Markdown format for external use (e.g., Anki or Notion).
